## 1. Overview

- **Designer**: Gary Arant, Justin Gary
- **Publisher**: Stone Blade Entertainment (Ultra Pro)
- **Year**: 2018
- **Players**: 2-4 (best at 2)
- **Play Time**: ~30 minutes
- **Genre**: Deck-building card game with direct combat

Shards of Infinity is a competitive deck-building game where players acquire cards from a shared market, build up their decks, gain Mastery, and attempt to reduce opponents' health to zero -- or achieve an infinite-damage win via the Infinity Shard.

---

## 2. Components

- 128 card main deck (the "Infinity Deck")
- 4 player starter decks (10 cards each, 40 cards total)
- 1 Infinity Shard card (per player -- each player starts with one)
- 4 player reference/health tracker cards
- Health tracking tokens or dials (50 HP per player)
- Mastery tracking tokens or dials (starts at 0)

---

## 3. Setup

1. Each player takes one **starter deck** of 10 cards:
   - 7 Crystals (each provides 1 Gem)
   - 2 Blaster cards (each provides 1 Power)
   - 1 Infinity Shard (unique card -- starts in deck, not separate)
2. Each player sets their **Health to 50** and **Mastery to 0**.
3. Shuffle the main Infinity Deck and place it face down as the draw pile.
4. Reveal the top **6 cards** from the Infinity Deck to form the **Center Row** (the shared market).
5. Each player shuffles their personal 10-card starter deck and draws **5 cards** as their starting hand.
6. Determine a starting player (random). Play proceeds clockwise.

**First player adjustment**: The first player draws only **3 cards** on their first turn (instead of 5) to offset the advantage of going first. (In a 2-player game this is the standard rule; multiplayer variants may differ.)

---

## 4. Turn Structure

A player's turn consists of the following phases:

### 4a. Play Phase
- Play any number of cards from your hand in any order.
- Cards generate **Gems**, **Power**, and/or **Mastery** when played.
- **Ally abilities** trigger if you have played (or have in play) another card of the same faction during this turn.
- Cards with **Mastery thresholds** grant bonus effects if your Mastery level meets or exceeds the threshold printed on the card.
- You may use accumulated Gems to **buy cards** from the Center Row at any time during this phase.
- You may **Focus** — spend 1 Gem to gain 1 Mastery (Character Focus; see §24b) — at any time during this phase.
- You may use accumulated Power to **attack opponents** or their Champions at any time during this phase.

### 4b. Buy Cards (part of Play Phase)
- Spend Gems to purchase cards from the Center Row.
- Purchased cards go to your **discard pile** (not your hand).
- Immediately refill the Center Row from the Infinity Deck so there are always 6 cards available.
- You may buy multiple cards per turn if you have enough Gems.

### 4c. Attack (part of Play Phase)
- Spend accumulated Power to deal damage to an opponent.
- If an opponent has a **Champion with a shield (guard)** in play, you must destroy that Champion before you can deal damage to the player directly.
- Champions without guard can be attacked optionally (you can bypass them to hit the player directly).
- When a player's health reaches 0, they are eliminated.

### 4d. Cleanup Phase
- All played cards (except Champions that remain in play) go to your discard pile.
- **Mercenaries** (one-shot cards) go to the center discard pile (removed from your deck) after being played -- they do NOT go to your personal discard pile.
- Unspent Gems, Power, and Mastery bonuses from this turn are lost (do not carry over) -- EXCEPT Mastery, which is tracked cumulatively on your Mastery dial/counter.
- Draw 5 new cards from your personal draw pile.
- If your draw pile is empty, shuffle your discard pile to form a new draw pile, then continue drawing.

---

## 5. Win Conditions

1. **Elimination**: Reduce all opponents' health to 0. Last player standing wins.
2. **Infinity Shard activation**: If you activate the Infinity Shard when your Mastery is at **30 or higher**, you deal **infinite damage** to all opponents, instantly winning the game.

---

## 6. Resources

### 6a. Gems (Currency)
- Used to purchase cards from the Center Row.
- Generated by playing cards that produce Gems.
- Unspent Gems are lost at end of turn.
- Starter deck Crystals each produce 1 Gem.

### 6b. Power (Damage/Attack)
- Used to deal damage to opponents or destroy opponent Champions.
- Generated by playing cards that produce Power.
- Unspent Power is lost at end of turn.
- Starter deck Blasters each produce 1 Power.

### 6c. Mastery (Experience/Level)
- A cumulative resource tracked on each player's Mastery dial.
- **Mastery never decreases** -- it only goes up.
- Gained by playing cards that grant Mastery.
- Mastery unlocks enhanced effects on cards that have Mastery thresholds.
- The critical threshold is **30 Mastery** -- this enables the Infinity Shard's instant-win ability.
- Mastery persists between turns (it is NOT reset).

---

## 7. Card Types

### 7a. Champions
- **Persist in play** between turns (they are NOT discarded at end of turn).
- Have a **health/shield value** -- opponents must spend Power equal to their shield to destroy them.
- Some Champions have **Guard** -- opponents MUST destroy them before attacking the player directly.
- Champions provide their abilities each turn they remain in play.
- When destroyed, Champions go to their owner's discard pile (they can be reshuffled and replayed later).

### 7b. Mercenaries
- **One-time use** cards.
- When played, they provide their effect for that turn only.
- After use, Mercenaries are **removed from the game** (placed in a shared discard/removed pile), NOT returned to your personal discard pile.
- This means Mercenaries thin your deck -- they don't clog it up.
- Mercenaries are generally more powerful than equivalent-cost permanent cards because they're single-use.

### 7c. Regular Cards (Actions/Items)
- The standard card type -- played from hand for their effects, then discarded to your personal discard pile at end of turn.
- Will be reshuffled and drawn again in future turns.
- These form the backbone of your deck's engine.

---

## 8. The Infinity Shard

- Every player starts with **one Infinity Shard** in their 10-card starter deck.
- When played, the Infinity Shard's effect depends on your current Mastery level:
  - **Mastery 0-4**: Gain 1 Mastery (the Shard helps you level up).
  - **Mastery 5-9**: Gain 1 Mastery and 1 Power.
  - **Mastery 10-14**: Gain 1 Mastery and additional Power (scales up).
  - **Mastery 15-19**: Even stronger effects.
  - **Mastery 20-29**: Very strong Power + Mastery gain.
  - **Mastery 30+**: **INFINITE DAMAGE** -- you instantly win the game by dealing infinite damage to all opponents.
- The Infinity Shard provides a powerful alternate win condition and creates urgency: if one player's Mastery is climbing fast, others must either race their own Mastery or try to eliminate that player before they reach 30.

> **Exact Infinity Shard scaling**: The precise Power values at each Mastery tier are documented in the card list section below. The key thresholds are roughly every 5 Mastery levels.

## 9. Factions / Colors

There are **four factions** in the base game, each with a distinct color and mechanical identity:

### 9a. Homodeus (Blue)
- **Theme**: Technology, knowledge, transcendence
- **Mechanical identity**: Mastery generation, card draw, deck manipulation
- **Strengths**: Fastest path to high Mastery, card advantage
- **Ally ability theme**: Draw cards, gain Mastery

### 9b. Wraethe (Red/Purple)
- **Theme**: Destruction, aggression, chaos
- **Mechanical identity**: Direct damage (Power), aggressive effects, opponent disruption
- **Strengths**: High Power output, fast elimination
- **Ally ability theme**: Bonus Power, forcing opponents to discard or lose health

### 9c. Order of the New Dawn (Gold/Yellow)
- **Theme**: Healing, defense, unity
- **Mechanical identity**: Health gain, shields, defensive Champions
- **Strengths**: Survivability, healing to outlast opponents
- **Ally ability theme**: Gain health, deploy defensive Champions

### 9d. Undergrowth (Green)
- **Theme**: Nature, growth, overwhelming force
- **Mechanical identity**: Gem generation, large creatures, ramping economy
- **Strengths**: Economic power, big late-game plays
- **Ally ability theme**: Bonus Gems, cost reduction

### Faction Synergies (Ally Abilities)
- Many cards have an **Ally Ability** printed on them.
- An Ally Ability triggers **only if** you play or control another card of the **same faction** during the same turn.
- This encourages building a deck focused on one or two factions for consistent Ally triggers.
- Ally abilities provide significant bonus effects (extra Power, Gems, Mastery, card draw, healing, etc.).

## 10. Center Row / Market

- The Center Row always contains **6 face-up cards** from the Infinity Deck.
- When a card is purchased or otherwise removed, immediately refill from the top of the Infinity Deck.
- Any player can buy any card in the Center Row on their turn, as long as they have enough Gems.
- The Center Row provides a shared, rotating market that all players compete over.
- There is no "always available" card (unlike some deck-builders like Star Realms which have an always-available basic card). Players can only buy from the 6 revealed cards.

## 11. Card Anatomy

Each card in Shards of Infinity has the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Name** | The card's name |
| **Cost** | Gem cost to purchase from the Center Row (top-left corner). Starter cards have no cost. |
| **Faction** | Color/faction symbol indicating which faction the card belongs to. Some cards may be factionless. |
| **Type** | Champion, Mercenary, or regular card |
| **Card Text / Abilities** | The primary effect when played |
| **Ally Ability** | Bonus effect that triggers when you play/control another card of the same faction (marked with faction icon) |
| **Mastery Threshold** | Some cards have a Mastery requirement -- if your Mastery meets or exceeds this number, you gain a bonus effect |
| **Shield Value** | Champions only -- the amount of Power needed to destroy this Champion |
| **Guard** | Champions only -- if present, opponents must destroy this Champion before attacking the player |

## 12. Player Interaction

- **Direct attack**: Spend Power to reduce an opponent's health.
- **Champion destruction**: Spend Power to destroy opponent Champions (required for Guard champions before hitting player).
- **In multiplayer (3-4 players)**: You can split your Power among different opponents/champions.
- **No trading**: Players cannot trade cards or resources.
- **Elimination**: When a player reaches 0 health, they are out of the game. Their cards are removed.
- **Target selection**: You choose which opponent(s) to attack. There is no mandatory target.

| Mastery Level | Effect |

| 5+ | Gain 1 Mastery, gain 3 Power |
| 10+ | Gain 1 Mastery, gain 6 Power |
| 15+ | Gain 1 Mastery, gain 10 Power |
| 20+ | Gain 1 Mastery, gain 15 Power |

> **Note on scaling values**: The exact Power numbers at each Mastery tier above are approximate reconstructions. The general pattern is confirmed -- the Shard gains increasing Power as Mastery rises, culminating in infinite damage at 30. ## 16. Champion Mechanics (Detailed)

### Deploying Champions
- Champions are played from your hand like any other card.
- They remain in play in front of you until destroyed by an opponent.
- A Champion's ongoing abilities activate each turn on YOUR turn (not opponents' turns).
- Champions count as being "in play" for ally ability purposes. A Champion from a prior turn that is still in play DOES count as an ally for newly played cards of the same faction.

### Guard Champions
- Guard is a keyword on some Champions (mostly Order of the New Dawn).
- When a player has one or more Guard champions in play, opponents **cannot deal damage directly to that player** until all Guard champions are destroyed.
- Multiple Guard champions stack -- all must be destroyed before the player can be attacked directly.
- Guard champions must be individually targeted and destroyed by spending Power equal to their Shield value.

### Destroying Champions
- An opponent spends Power equal to the Champion's Shield value to destroy it.
- You must spend the **exact or greater** amount -- partial damage does NOT carry over (you cannot chip away at a Champion across multiple turns unless there is a specific card that allows it).
- Destroyed Champions go to their **owner's discard pile** and can be replayed later when drawn again.
- You CAN choose to attack a non-Guard Champion instead of the player, if you wish (tactical choice to remove their engine piece).

### Champion Timing
- You play a Champion from your hand during your Play Phase.
- It immediately enters play and you can use its ability that same turn.
- It stays in play through your opponents' turns.
- On your next turn, its ability triggers again.
- Champions that generate resources (Gems, Power, Mastery) do so each turn they are in play.

## 17. Mastery System (Detailed)

### Accumulation
- Mastery is tracked on a separate dial/counter for each player.
- Mastery starts at 0 and can only increase -- it never decreases.
- Multiple cards can grant Mastery in a single turn -- they all add up.
- There is no maximum Mastery (other than the practical cap where 30+ wins you the game via Infinity Shard).

### Mastery Threshold Effects
- Many cards in the Infinity Deck have a **Mastery threshold** printed on them.
- Format: "Mastery X+: [bonus effect]"
- If your current Mastery level meets or exceeds X when you play the card, you gain the bonus effect IN ADDITION to the card's normal effect.
- Common thresholds are 5, 10, and 15.
- This creates a scaling power curve -- cards you bought early become MORE powerful as your Mastery increases.

### Strategic Importance
- Mastery creates a "clock" in the game -- as any player approaches 30, the game becomes urgent.
- Players must balance between Mastery investment and direct power/economy.
- Homodeus faction is the primary Mastery-acceleration faction, but all factions have some Mastery sources.
- The Infinity Shard itself grants 1 Mastery each time it's played, providing a baseline accumulation.

## 18. Edge Cases and FAQ

### Q: Can I play cards in any order?
**A**: Yes. You may play cards in any order during your Play Phase. This matters for ally abilities -- playing a faction card first means subsequent cards of that faction will trigger their ally abilities.

### Q: Do ally abilities trigger from Champions already in play?
**A**: Yes. If you have a Champion of a certain faction already in play from a previous turn, any new card of that same faction you play this turn will have its ally ability activated. The Champion counts as an ally.

### Q: Can I buy a card and play it immediately?
**A**: No. Purchased cards go to your discard pile. You will draw them in a future turn.

### Q: What happens when the Infinity Deck runs out?
**A**: If the Infinity Deck runs out, the Center Row is not refilled. Players continue playing with whatever cards are available. The game does not end when the deck runs out.

### Q: Can health go above 50?
**A**: Yes. There is no maximum health cap. Healing effects can raise health above 50.

### Q: Can I choose not to attack?
**A**: Yes. Unspent Power is simply lost at end of turn. You are never forced to attack.

### Q: What if multiple Guard champions are in play?
**A**: You can choose which Guard champion to attack first, but you must destroy ALL Guard champions before dealing damage to the player directly.

### Q: Can I split damage between a Champion and a player?
**A**: No. You must fully destroy a Champion (spending Power equal to its Shield) or not attack it at all. Excess Power after destroying a Champion CAN be used to attack the player or another Champion. But you cannot partially damage a Champion.

### Q: When does Mastery from a card take effect?
**A**: Immediately when the card is played. If a card grants Mastery and also has a Mastery threshold, the Mastery is gained FIRST, which could potentially unlock the threshold bonus on that same card. (Verify this -- some rulings may differ.)

### Q: Do Mercenaries trigger ally abilities?
**A**: Yes. Mercenaries have a faction and count as allies for the turn they are played, just like any other card. They trigger ally abilities and can have their own ally abilities triggered.

### Q: What happens when a player is eliminated in multiplayer?
**A**: The eliminated player removes all their cards from the game (hand, deck, discard pile, and any Champions in play). Play continues with remaining players.

### Q: Can the Infinity Shard be removed from my deck?
**A**: No. The Infinity Shard is a permanent part of your deck and cannot be trashed, removed, or given away by any game effect.

### Q: Is there a hand size limit?
**A**: No. You always draw 5 cards at the end of your turn, but card draw effects during your turn can result in holding more than 5 cards. There is no maximum hand size.

### Q: Can I choose not to use Gems/not buy anything?
**A**: Yes. Unspent Gems are lost at end of turn, but you are never forced to buy.

## 19. Multiplayer Rules (3-4 Players)

- The game supports 2-4 players with the same core rules.
- In 3-4 player games:
  - All players start with 50 health.
  - The Center Row is still 6 cards.
  - Players take turns clockwise.
  - You can split Power among multiple opponents and/or their Champions.
  - The game ends when only one player remains (or someone triggers the Infinity Shard at 30+ Mastery).
  - Eliminated players are fully removed from the game.
  - The first player still draws fewer cards on their first turn.
- **Political dynamics**: Multiplayer adds negotiation and politics -- players may choose to gang up on a Mastery leader or leave a weakened player alone.

## 21. Detailed Turn Flow (Implementation Reference)

For a developer implementing this game, here is the precise turn flow as a state machine:

```
START_TURN:
  1. (No automatic untap/refresh -- Champions stay as-is)
  2. Player enters PLAY_PHASE

PLAY_PHASE (loop until player ends turn):
  Player may do any of the following in any order, any number of times:
  
  a. PLAY_CARD(card):
     - Remove card from hand
     - If Champion: place in play area (stays between turns)
     - If Regular/Mercenary: place in "played this turn" area
     - Resolve card effects:
       * Add Gems to gem_pool
       * Add Power to power_pool
       * Add Mastery to mastery_counter (permanent)
       * Execute special effects (draw cards, heal, etc.)
     - Check ally condition: if another card of same faction is
       in play or was played this turn, trigger ally ability
     - Check mastery threshold: if player.mastery >= threshold,
       trigger mastery bonus effect
  
  b. BUY_CARD(center_row_card):
     - Verify gem_pool >= card.cost
     - Subtract card.cost from gem_pool
     - Move card from center_row to player.discard_pile
     - Refill center_row from infinity_deck (if cards remain)
  
  c. ATTACK(target_player_or_champion, amount):
     - Verify power_pool >= amount
     - If target is a player:
       * Verify target has no Guard champions in play
       * Subtract amount from power_pool
       * Subtract amount from target.health
       * If target.health <= 0: eliminate target
     - If target is a champion:
       * Verify amount >= champion.shield (must destroy in one shot)
       * Subtract champion.shield from power_pool
       * Move champion to owner.discard_pile
  
  d. END_TURN: proceed to CLEANUP

CLEANUP:
  1. Move all Regular cards from "played this turn" to player.discard_pile
  2. Move all Mercenaries from "played this turn" to removed_from_game pile
  3. Reset gem_pool to 0
  4. Reset power_pool to 0
  5. (Mastery counter is NOT reset)
  6. Player draws 5 cards from their draw_pile
     - If draw_pile has fewer than 5 cards:
       a. Draw remaining cards from draw_pile
       b. Shuffle discard_pile into new draw_pile
       c. Draw remaining needed cards
  7. Next player's turn begins
```

## 23. Timing and Resolution Rules

### Order of Operations
1. When a card is played, its **base effects resolve first**.
2. Then check for **Mastery threshold** -- if met, resolve bonus.
3. Then check for **Ally ability** -- if an ally is present, resolve bonus.
4. Mastery gained from the card's base effect IS counted before checking that same card's Mastery threshold. (This means a card that grants Mastery could push you over its own threshold.)

### Simultaneous Effects
- If multiple effects happen at once, the active player chooses the order.
- Draw effects resolve immediately -- drawn cards can be played in the same turn.

### "When Played" vs "While in Play"
- Regular cards and Mercenaries have "when played" effects -- they trigger once.
- Champions have abilities that trigger **each turn** the Champion is in play, at the start of the controller's turn (effectively during the Play Phase when the player chooses to activate them).

> **Note**: There is some ambiguity about whether Champion abilities trigger automatically at the start of your turn or whether you "activate" them during your play phase. For implementation purposes, treat Champion abilities as automatically providing their resources/effects at the start of each of the owner's turns.

## 24. Special Card Effects Reference

Some cards have effects beyond simple resource generation. Here are the types of special effects a developer must support:

| Effect Type | Description | Example |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Draw cards** | Draw N cards from your draw pile into your hand | "Draw 2 cards" |
| **Gain Health** | Increase your health by N | "Gain 5 Health" |
| **Opponent loses Health** | Direct health loss (not Power-based, bypasses Guard) | "Target opponent loses 3 Health" |
| **Banish/Remove** | Remove a card from the game permanently | "Banish a card from your hand or discard pile" |
| **Scrap from Center Row** | Remove a card from the Center Row without buying it | "Scrap a card in the Center Row" |
| **Choose one** | Pick one of multiple effects | "Gain 2 Gems OR Gain 2 Power" |
| **Conditional Power** | Power that scales based on game state | "Gain 1 Power for each Champion you control" |

### Banish/Scrap Mechanic
- Some cards allow you to **banish** (permanently remove) a card from your hand or discard pile.
- This is a deck-thinning mechanic -- you can remove weak starter cards (Crystals, Blasters) to increase the average power of your draws.
- Banished cards go to the removed-from-game pile and never return.
- Scrapping from the Center Row lets you deny opponents access to a card they might want, while also refreshing the market.

## 24b. Expansion Mechanics (Implemented in This Engine)

> Scope note: per the project's multiplayer-only scope, **competitive** expansion
> content (including Destiny) is in scope; only co-op content is excluded. The
> following three mechanics are implemented in `GameService` and are part of the
> live rules, not just the base game.

### Character Focus (gem → mastery)

Each player has a **Character** (a chosen identity, e.g. `convergence`,
`fervor`). The **Focus** action lets the active player convert economy into
Mastery: spend **1 Gem to gain 1 Mastery**. In the engine this is
`GameService.focus()` — it requires 1 gem in the pool, deducts it, adds 1
mastery, and sets a per-turn `focusedThisTurn` flag (surfaced over the network in
the redacted view). Focus is a deliberate Mastery-acceleration lever: a player
sitting on spare gems can pivot them toward the 30-Mastery Infinity-Shard clock
instead of buying cards.

### Destiny (Into the Horizon)

Destinies are a **separate supply**, not part of the center-row market. At setup
the engine shuffles the Destiny cards and deals six face-up into a shared
**Destiny row** (`GameService.destinyRow`); the rest form the **Destiny deck**
(`destinyDeck`, the cascade source). The row is **not** auto-refilled on claim.

- **Claim** (`claimDestiny`): the active player takes a face-up Destiny from the
  shared row **for free** into their personal `PlayerState.claimedDestinies` zone
  (it never enters their draw pile). Typically gated behind a Mastery threshold
  (claimed at Mastery 5 in the physical game).
- **Use** (`useDestinyAbility`): a claimed Destiny's ability is used **per turn**
  (like an activated/Exhaust ability) rather than being shuffled into the deck.
- **Cascade** (`banishDestinyToCascade`): a Destiny can be banished to advance /
  cascade through the Destiny deck.

The Destiny supply is built from the authoritative card DB via
`buildDestinySupplyFromDatabase` (the `Destiny` / `DestinyDeck` groups, ~30 cards,
one copy each — Destinies are unique) and is explicitly excluded from the market
deck.

### Relics (Relics of the Future)

Relics are character-specific permanent cards set aside at game start. At a
Mastery milestone (Mastery 10 in the physical game) a player **recruits ONE of
their Character's two relics for free** (`GameService.recruitRelic`); the other is
banished. The chosen relic is **shuffled into the player's draw pile** so it comes
up like any other card. The two candidates live in `PlayerState.relicOptions`,
populated from the `Character → [offensive, defensive] relic ids` map in
`lib/data/character_relics.dart`. Characters without a confirmed relic pair
(`rez` / `chroma`) recruit to a no-op.

## 26. Strategy Context (For AI/Balancing Reference)

Understanding strategic archetypes helps ensure the game is implemented correctly:

### Rush Strategy (Wraethe-heavy)
- Focus on Power-generating cards early.
- Aim to eliminate opponents before Mastery becomes relevant.
- Weakness: loses to heavy healing/Guard if the game goes long.

### Mastery Rush (Homodeus-heavy)
- Focus on Mastery-generating cards.
- Race to 30 Mastery for the Infinity Shard win.
- Weakness: low combat power makes you vulnerable to aggression.

### Turtle/Heal (Order-heavy)
- Focus on healing and Guard champions.
- Outlast opponents while slowly building Mastery.
- Weakness: can be outpaced by a dedicated Mastery rush.

### Economy Engine (Undergrowth-heavy)
- Build a strong Gem economy to buy expensive, powerful cards.
- Transition to power/mastery in the late game.
- Weakness: slow start can be punished by early aggression.

### Hybrid Strategies
- Most winning strategies combine 2 factions for ally synergies.
- Pure single-faction is hard because the Center Row is random.
- Flexibility and reading what's available is key.

### Known Gaps Remaining

1. **Exact card list**: The card names, stats, and effects in Section 15 are reconstructions, not transcriptions. Many are likely close but should be verified against the actual game.
2. **Card copy counts**: How many copies of each card exist in the 128-card Infinity Deck is unknown.
3. **Infinity Shard exact Power values**: The scaling numbers need verification against the physical card.
4. **First player draw count**: Confirmed as reduced, but the exact count (3 vs 4) for different player counts needs verification.
5. **Some special card effects**: Cards with unique/complex effects may exist that are not captured in the card list.
6. **Official errata or FAQ**: No official FAQ was accessible; some rulings may have been updated by the publisher.
7. **Digital app differences**: The official Shards of Infinity digital app (by Temple Gates Games) may have slight rule clarifications that differ from the physical rulebook.

### Recommendation for Developer

For a faithful implementation, the developer should:
1. **Acquire the physical game** or official digital app to verify the card list.
2. **Cross-reference** the card list in Section 15 with the actual cards -- use this document as a structural template and fill in exact values.
3. **Check the official rulebook PDF** (available from Stone Blade Entertainment's website) for any rules this document may have slightly wrong.
4. **Test edge cases** from Section 18 against the official app's behavior.
5. **Start with the core engine** (turn flow, resources, card types) which is high-confidence, then layer in specific cards.
